# Meaning sources

The headword definitions are based on the Turkish dictionary snapshot distributed by [bora-7/tdk-12-kindle](https://github.com/bora-7/tdk-12-kindle), `json/tdk-12.json.zip`, blob `42c7919958b32043fe85bb96128cbb143caa1085` (repository MIT license). Duplicate senses were merged and dictionary cross references were expanded where possible. Existing KAPMACA geographic, nationality and imperative meanings are retained. Inflected forms cite the corresponding dictionary stem. Supplemental common-word explanations are maintained by KAPMACA.

RA is retained for its independent mythological meaning: [British Museum, Ra](https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/term/BIOG55511). AR includes its Turkish sense of shame, honour and modesty. Periodic-table-only meanings are excluded.

Definition JSON files are loaded only when a player opens a word meaning. The playable headword list and these files are released together.
